Delete your GlideTile account

App: GlideTile on Google Play (package com.yehuda.glidetile) · Developer: Yehuda P. (TerraVectors) · Contact: yehuda.g.p@gmail.com

You have a GlideTile account only if you opened Play with friends or Play online, which signs you in with Google Play Games. Playing puzzles alone needs no account.

In the game (fastest)

Open GlideTile → Credits → Delete my account → Delete. It happens at once.

Also: anything our server keeps for an account that has not played online for 90 days is deleted automatically.

Or by e-mail

  1. Send an e-mail to yehuda.g.p@gmail.com with the subject "GlideTile - delete my account".
  2. Send it from the Google account you play with, or write your Play Games player name in the message.
  3. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by e-mail.

What is deleted

  • The sign-in record of your account in Firebase Authentication (account identifier and sign-in dates).
  • Everything our game server keeps for your account: player name, Play Games player identifier, friends, recent players, invitations, notification token, language, league and rating, and your invitation code and the friends who joined by it.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Anonymous game statistics (a random number made on the phone, not linked to your account) are not tied to you, so we cannot pick them out; they are deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Purchases ("Remove Ads") are kept by Google Play under your Google account, not by us.
  • Coins, scores and settings are stored only on your phone; uninstall the app or clear its data to remove them.
  • Usage statistics in Google Analytics for Firebase are pseudonymous and kept for the period set by Google.
