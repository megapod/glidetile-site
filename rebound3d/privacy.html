Rebound 3D - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026. Developer: TerraVectors. Contact: terravectors@gmail.com

What this version collects

The current test version of Rebound 3D (package com.terravectors.rebound3d) collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and it does not connect to the internet. Your game progress stays on your device.

Google Play

Google Play may collect installation and crash information under Google's own privacy policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

When a later version adds online play, advertising or accounts, this page will be updated before that version is released, and it will list exactly what is collected and why.

Deleting your data

This version stores nothing outside your device. Uninstalling the game removes everything. See also delete-data.html.
