Delete your GlideTile data (keep your account)

App: GlideTile on Google Play (package com.yehuda.glidetile) · Developer: Yehuda P. (TerraVectors) · Contact: yehuda.g.p@gmail.com

You can ask us to delete some or all of the data our game server keeps for your account, and keep playing with the same account.

How to ask

  1. Send an e-mail to yehuda.g.p@gmail.com with the subject "GlideTile - delete my data".
  2. Send it from the Google account you play with, or write your Play Games player name in the message.
  3. Say what to delete (any of the list below), or "all". We delete within 30 days and confirm by e-mail.

What you can have deleted

  • Your friends list and recent players.
  • Pending invitations and your notification token (you will stop receiving invitation notifications).
  • Your league and rating (you start again from the first league).
  • Your invitation code and the record of friends who joined by it.
  • Your player name as stored on our server (the game shows your Play Games name again the next time you play online).

What is kept

  • Your account itself (sign-in record and account identifier), so you can keep playing. To remove it too, see Delete your account.
  • Anonymous game statistics are not linked to your account, so we cannot pick them out; they are deleted automatically after 12 months.
  • Coins, scores and settings live only on your phone; clear the app's data to remove them.
