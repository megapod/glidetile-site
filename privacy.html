Privacy Policy — GlideTile

App: GlideTile (package com.yehuda.glidetile) · Developer: Yehuda P. · Contact: yehuda.g.p@gmail.com · Last updated: September 11, 2026

Overview

GlideTile is a sliding-tile puzzle game. This policy explains what data is processed when you play, and by whom. The short version: we do not ask for, collect, or store any personal information on our own servers. The game has no account system. The only data processing happens on your device, and by Google services that power ads.

Data stored on your device only

Your best times, move counts, and local high-score leaderboard entries (including the player name you type in for a new record) are saved locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not uploaded to us or to any server. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Custom photos

GlideTile lets you choose a photo from your device to use as a puzzle image. This photo is read from your device, used to build the puzzle tiles entirely on your device, and is never uploaded, transmitted, or stored anywhere by us. It is not saved by the app beyond the current game session.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, and ad-interaction events. This processing is governed by Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from ads

Where required by law (for example in the EEA/UK), you will be shown a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before personalized ads are served, and you may choose non-personalized ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device's Google settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Children

GlideTile is not directed at children under 13, and is rated accordingly on Google Play. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through our app, contact us and we will address it.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal data. We do not share data with any third party other than the Google services described above, which operate under their own policies.

Data retention and deletion

We hold no personal data on our servers, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Local data (best times, leaderboard entries, any name you typed in) is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Changes to this policy

Future versions of the game may add features (such as an online leaderboard) that change what data is processed. This page will be updated before any such change takes effect, and the "last updated" date will reflect it.
